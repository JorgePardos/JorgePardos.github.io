03— Projects

Things I've built

Personal Python side-projects that grew out of my own research — small in scope, but built with tests and documentation so someone else could actually run them.

PenIndex

from my own paper

Interaction analyzer implementing the Penetration Index, a purely geometrical bonding descriptor I co-developed and published in Dalton Transactions (2026). Detects and classifies covalent, hydrogen-bond, van der Waals and metallophilic contacts across structures and MD trajectories.

A single distance stands in for energy-decomposition analyses too expensive to run at scale — useful for screening bonding character across many structures at once.

PythonMDAnalysispytestPyMOL

md-interactions

Pip-installable CLI that reproducibly analyzes enzyme–substrate MD trajectories: catalytic distances/angles, hydrogen bonds, RMSD/RMSF, free-energy maps and clustering. Validates the full configuration against the topology before running a single frame, then outputs publication-ready figures and a report.

Built to replace the one-off scripts a lab reruns by hand each time — something a colleague, not just its author, can actually pick up and run.

PythonCLIpytestMatplotlib

Molecule2input

End-to-end pipeline from a hand-drawn or ChemDraw-style molecule image to a ready-to-run Gaussian/ORCA input file. Combines deep-learning structure recognition (DECIMER, MolScribe) with deterministic, RDKit-based stereochemistry, charge/spin assignment and 3D embedding — plus a human verification step at the one point the pipeline is uncertain.

PythonRDKitDeep learningCLI

frame_extractor

A focused utility that replicates AMBER's cpptraj frame/trajectory extraction: centers the solute, wraps solvent across periodic boundaries, and exports any frame or range using familiar cpptraj-style indexing.

PythonMDAnalysisParmEd

05— Experience

Research experience

PhD Researcher — Computational Chemistry

2023 – Present
Universidad de Zaragoza
  • Elucidating the catalytic mechanism of EarP, a bacterial enzyme with no human counterpart that modifies elongation factor EF-P — a modification linked to virulence and antibiotic resistance in pathogens such as Pseudomonas aeruginosa, and studied here as a potential selective antibacterial target. Used QM/MM, molecular dynamics and metadynamics, cross-validated against experimental mutagenesis data.
  • Developed the Penetration Index, a geometrical bonding descriptor correlating with independently computed covalency descriptors — a cheaper proxy for bonding character than the energy-decomposition methods it was validated against; published in Dalton Transactions.
  • Replaced ad-hoc analysis scripts with reusable, tested Python packages and automated workflows for multiscale simulations on Linux/HPC infrastructure.

Erasmus Mundus MSc Researcher

2021 – 2023
Universidad de Valencia / Universidad Autónoma de Madrid
  • Master's thesis: resolved a long-standing mechanistic controversy for GTP hydrolysis by RhoA — a Ras-superfamily GTPase implicated in breast, colon and lung cancers, and an active target for small-molecule inhibitor design — using QM/MM free-energy calculations.
  • Identified glutamine tautomerism as the rate-limiting proton-transfer step, matching the experimental rate constant and reconciling several competing mechanistic proposals from the literature; published in ACS Catalysis.
  • Trained in advanced electronic-structure theory and computational modelling across a competitive, multi-institution European programme (EMTCCM).
